The Opportunity in India

India is at the precipice of an energy revolution. By redirecting agricultural residue away from harmful crop burning and into our pelletization infrastructure, we resolve two massive challenges simultaneously.

The Vision & Leadership

Meet the Founder

Inspired by the growing need for sustainable industrial fuels, founder Krishna Prajapati established KBM with a mission to build a reliable ecosystem connecting local suppliers with heavy industrial consumers.

KBM is currently in active development. We are identifying premium feedstock suppliers, evaluating state-of-the-art extrusion machinery, and securing strategic industrial partnerships in Indore and across the region.

Our Long-Term Objective

To establish a modern, scalable biomass pellet production facility capable of supplying reliable, continuous, and sustainable fuel solutions across the nation.
